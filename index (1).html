Roles & Permissions

The Employee role sees every tab except the ones marked Admin-only below. Tick a box to also let Employee open that tab. This does not change what SalesRep sees (a separate, fixed order-taking screen), and it never grants access to the account-management or data-control tools on this page or in the top header — those stay Admin-only.

Admin-only pages

Detailed feature permissions

Choose exactly which pages and actions the Employee role can use: adding stock, viewing or deleting bills, stock audit, payments, printing and more. Unticked features are hidden from Employees and refused if they try to use them. The default tick marks match what Employees could already do, so nothing changes until you untick (or, for the ones marked Admin-only by default, tick) something. A whole page must also be allowed under "Page Access" (or "Admin-only pages" above) for its actions to be reachable. Settings, Switch Role, Backup, Restore and Reset always stay Admin-only.

User-wise Permissions (per login)

Give one specific Employee login its own pages and actions. Pick a login, tick Use custom permissions for this login, choose what that person may use, then save. Logins without custom permissions keep following the Roles & Permissions panel above. Admin accounts always have full access, and Settings, Switch Role, Backup, Restore and Reset always stay Admin-only. The permissions apply the next time that person logs in.
